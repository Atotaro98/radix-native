`TextField.Slot`: icons or buttons inside a TextField, like Radix Themes (`side`, `color`, `gap`, `px` / `pl` / `pr`). The first slot goes on the left and a second one on the right; icons without a `color` take the slot's, and a press on a slot focuses the input. `TextField.Root` is an alias of `TextField`.
